# Sample-efficient VLM-targeted phase frontier

This proposal layer complements, but does not weaken, the registered staged
frontier gate.

- The frozen VLM sees hash-pinned matched telemetry and media from the current
  qualified search incumbent. It may nominate one complete target schedule;
  no Cartesian schedule grid is generated.
- The VLM receipt freezes its model identity, prompt hash, evidence hash,
  per-phase safe-success priors, per-phase evidence, and target schedule before
  any target outcome is opened.
- The complete target runs on exactly three distinct discovery poses. `3/3`
  only advances it to the ordinary fresh-seed `5 -> 10 -> 20` gate; it is not a
  reliability result.
- If discovery is imperfect, matched telemetry must identify the earliest
  physical divergence. Exactly that phase is lowered by one registered rung;
  every other phase remains fixed. An unclassified safety or physics event
  halts the lane.
- Discovery, staged-gate, and final seeds are disjoint. The untouched 100-seed
  final bank is opened only after search terminates with a qualified incumbent.
- VLM probabilities are acquisition priors only. Selection and reporting use
  measured success and matched-native speedup, with all rollout receipts
  counted exactly once.
